# Warranty FAQ (2019)

Answers to the most common warranty questions from Nimbus Bikes riders.

How long is the frame warranty on a Nimbus bike?
The frame warranty on every Nimbus bike lasts 5 years from the date of purchase. The fork is covered by the frame warranty for the same 5 years.

How long is the warranty on the e-bike battery?
The e-bike battery warranty lasts 2 years or 500 full charge cycles, whichever comes first. Charge cycles beyond 500 are not covered by the battery warranty.

How many days do I have to register my bike for the warranty?
You have 30 days to register your bike for the warranty at nimbusbikes.example/register. If you do not register your bike within 30 days, the warranty covers 1 year only.

Are components covered by the warranty?
Yes. Components are covered by the warranty for 1 year.

How do I make a warranty claim?
Call the warranty desk at 1-800-555-0199 with your receipt. Warranty claims are reviewed within 30 business days.

Is the warranty transferable?
No. The warranty covers the original owner of the bike only.
