# Returns and Refunds

Updated January 15, 2024.

We want you to love your ride. If a bike or accessory is not right for you, you can return it under the terms below.

## Return window

You can return a bike within 30 days of the delivery date. The bike must be in original condition with less than 25 miles (40 km) on it, or wear consistent with a short test ride. Accessories and apparel can also be returned within 30 days if they are unused and in the original packaging.

## Fees

There is no restocking fee for standard bikes. Nimbus Volt e-bikes carry a 15% restocking fee because the battery must be tested and recertified before resale. A prepaid return label for a boxed bike costs $65 and is deducted from your refund. Accessory returns ship free with the label included in your order email.

## Refund timing

Once the returned item reaches our Portland warehouse, our team inspects it within 2 business days. Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 7 business days after inspection. Your bank may take a few extra days to show the credit.

## Exchanges

Need a different frame size? Size exchanges within 30 days are free, including shipping, as long as the bike meets the return condition rules. We ship the replacement as soon as the original is scanned by the carrier.

## Items that cannot be returned

Custom builds, bikes bought from the clearance section, gift cards, and used bikes purchased through the trade-in program are final sale. Helmets that have been worn cannot be returned for safety reasons.
