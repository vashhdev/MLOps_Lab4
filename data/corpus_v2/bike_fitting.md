# Precision Bike Fitting

A good fit makes every ride more comfortable and more efficient. Our Precision Fit service is available at the Portland and Seattle stores.

## What is included

A Precision Fit session lasts 90 minutes. Your fitter measures flexibility and riding posture, then adjusts saddle height and setback, handlebar reach and drop, and cleat position. We use pressure mapping on the saddle and video motion capture to check knee tracking at different cadences.

## Price

A Precision Fit costs $150. If you buy a new Nimbus bike within 30 days of your fit, the full $150 is credited toward the bike. One follow-up adjustment within 60 days is included at no extra cost.

## Our fitters

Our lead fitter is Marisol Ortega, who has fitted more than 3,000 riders since joining Nimbus Bikes in 2016. She is a former collegiate track cyclist and runs the training program for every new fitter at Nimbus Bikes. Fitters in Seattle are trained in Portland and use the same equipment and process.

## Booking

Book a fit online or by calling the store. Bring your bike, your cycling shoes and the shorts you usually ride in. Please arrive 10 minutes early to complete a short questionnaire about past injuries and riding goals. If you need to cancel, let us know at least 24 hours ahead so another rider can take the slot.
