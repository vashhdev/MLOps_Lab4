# 2024 Product Lineup

Nimbus Bikes designs five models, and every one of them is assembled in Portland.

## Adult bikes

- Cirrus (road): A carbon road bike weighing 8.2 kg in size 54, with 12-speed electronic shifting and hydraulic disc brakes. Price: $1,899.
- Stratus (gravel): An aluminum gravel bike with clearance for 45 mm tires, mounts for three bottles and front and rear racks. The Stratus gravel bike costs $2,299.
- Altus (touring): A steel touring bike rated for 25 kg of rack load, with a dynamo hub and integrated lights. Price: $1,650.
- Nimbus Volt (e-bike): A city e-bike with a 250 W rear hub motor, a 630 Wh battery and pedal assist up to 25 km/h. Price: $3,499.

## Kids

- Cumulus Kids: 20-inch wheels for riders aged 6 to 9, with a lightweight aluminum frame and short-reach brake levers. Price: $349.

## Sizes and colors

Adult models come in five frame sizes (XS to XL). Every model is offered in Storm Grey and Sky Blue, and the Cirrus also comes in a limited Sunset Orange. Test rides are available at all three stores without an appointment; bring a photo ID.

## Accessories

Each new adult bike includes a free bottle cage and a set of pedals. Fenders, racks and lights designed for each model are sold separately in store and online.
