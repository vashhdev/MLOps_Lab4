# Service Menu and Repair Prices (2021)

The Nimbus Bikes repair price list for our service department.

Basic tune-up cost: A basic tune-up costs $59 and includes gear and brake adjustment.

Full overhaul price: The price of a full overhaul is $149. Cables and housing are not included in the overhaul price.

Hydraulic brake bleed cost: It costs $35 per brake to bleed hydraulic brakes.

Flat tire repair cost: A flat tire repair costs $10 labor plus the cost of the tube.

Wheel truing cost: Wheel truing costs $25 per wheel.

How long does a standard repair take? A standard repair takes 5 business days. Rush service is not available.

Prices in this service menu apply to the Portland shop only, and Seattle prices may vary. Bring your bike in during shop hours, no appointment needed. Payment for the repair is due at pickup. Bikes left more than 30 days after the repair is complete may be sold to cover the cost of the work.
