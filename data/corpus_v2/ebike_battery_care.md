# E-Bike Battery Care Guide

This guide covers the 630 Wh battery used on the Nimbus Volt e-bike. A little care keeps the battery healthy for many seasons.

## Charging

Use only the included 4A Nimbus charger. A full charge of the Nimbus Volt battery takes 4.5 hours from empty, and charging from 0 to 80% takes about 3 hours. The charger LED turns green when charging is complete. Do not charge the battery below 0°C or above 40°C. For daily riding, keeping the battery between 20% and 80% extends its lifespan.

## Range

Expect a range of 40 to 90 km per charge. Range depends on the assist mode (Eco, Tour or Boost), rider weight, temperature, terrain and tire pressure. Cold weather can reduce range by up to 30%.

## Storage

For storage longer than two weeks, store the battery at 50% charge in a dry place between 10°C and 25°C. Check the charge every 2 months during winter storage and top it up to 50% if it drops below 30%. Never leave the battery on the charger for weeks at a time.

## Replacement and recycling

A replacement Nimbus Volt battery costs $549 and can be fitted at any Nimbus Bikes store in about 15 minutes. Old batteries can be dropped off at any store for free recycling through our ReCycle program.

## Cleaning

Remove the battery before washing the bike. Wipe the contacts with a dry cloth and never use a pressure washer on the battery, the charging port or the motor.
