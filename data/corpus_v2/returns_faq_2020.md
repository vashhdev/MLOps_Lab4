# Returns FAQ (2020)

Quick answers about returning a bike to Nimbus Bikes.

How many days do I have to return a bike?
You have 14 days to return a bike. The return window for a bike starts on the day you place your order, not the delivery date.

Is there a restocking fee for returning an e-bike?
Yes. The restocking fee for returning an e-bike is 20% of the purchase price. Standard bikes have a 10% restocking fee.

How long does it take to receive my refund?
Your refund takes up to 14 business days after we receive your return. Refunds are issued as store credit unless you ask for a refund to your card.

Can I return a bike I have ridden?
Only if the bike has been ridden less than 10 miles.

Who pays for return shipping?
Customers pay for return shipping on all bikes and accessories. We recommend insuring the package for the full value of the bike.

Can I exchange my bike for a different size?
Size exchanges are treated as a return plus a new order, so the restocking fee applies.
