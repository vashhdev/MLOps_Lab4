# Customer Support

Our support team is based in Portland and is happy to help with orders, sizing, repairs and anything else about your bike.

## Contact options

- Phone: The customer support phone number is 1-800-555-0142, open Monday to Friday from 7 am to 6 pm Pacific time.
- Live chat: Available every day from 8 am to 8 pm Pacific time on nimbusbikes.example.
- Email: Write to support@nimbusbikes.example and we will reply within 24 hours, including weekends.

## Orders

You can change or cancel an order within 2 hours of placing it. After that, the order is usually packed and you will need to use the returns process instead. Your order confirmation email includes a link to track the status of your order at any time.

## Spare parts

Spare parts are ordered by the serial number of your bike, which is stamped under the bottom bracket. Our support team can confirm the right part before you buy.

## Escalations

If you are not happy with how an issue was handled, ask for an escalation. A support manager reviews every escalation within 3 business days and contacts you directly by phone or email.
