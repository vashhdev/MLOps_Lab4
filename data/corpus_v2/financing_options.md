# Financing Options

Nimbus Bikes offers financing through our lending partner, Ridewell Credit. You can apply online at checkout or in any store.

## Plans

- 12 months at 0% APR on purchases of $800 or more.
- The 24-month financing plan has a 7.99% APR and is available on purchases of $800 or more.
- 36 months at 9.99% APR on purchases over $2,000, such as the Nimbus Volt.

## Applying

Applications take about 2 minutes and use a soft credit check, so applying does not affect your credit score. You will see your approved amount and plan options before you accept anything. You must be 18 or older and a resident of the United States.

## Payments

The minimum monthly payment is $35. There is no prepayment penalty, so you can pay off your balance early at any time. Payments are made directly to Ridewell Credit through their app or website, not to Nimbus Bikes.

## Returns on financed purchases

If you return a financed bike under our returns policy, Ridewell Credit cancels the loan and refunds any payments you have already made. Rewards points are not earned on the financed portion of a purchase until the loan is fully paid.
