# Nimbus Bikes Warranty Policy

Effective date: March 1, 2024. This policy replaces all earlier warranty documents and FAQs.

Every Nimbus Bikes bicycle is assembled in Portland, Oregon and inspected by two technicians before it leaves the workshop. We stand behind that work with the coverage below. Coverage applies to the original owner only and is not transferable when a bike is sold.

## Coverage periods

- Frame and fork: The frame warranty on adult models (Cirrus, Stratus, Altus and Nimbus Volt) lasts 10 years from the date of purchase and covers defects in materials and workmanship. Cumulus Kids frames are covered for 3 years.
- Components: Derailleurs, brakes, wheels, handlebars, seatposts and other original components are covered for 2 years.
- E-bike battery and motor: The Nimbus Volt battery warranty lasts 3 years or 800 full charge cycles, whichever comes first, and also covers the hub motor. The battery management system logs every cycle, so our service team can read the count during a claim.
- Paint and decals: Covered for 1 year against peeling and fading.

## Registration

To activate full coverage, register your bike within 60 days of purchase at nimbusbikes.example/register. You will need the serial number stamped under the bottom bracket. Bikes that are not registered in time receive only 1 year of coverage on every part.

## Making a claim

Email warranty@nimbusbikes.example with your proof of purchase, the serial number and clear photos of the problem. A warranty specialist will reply with a decision within 10 business days. Approved repairs are free, including shipping both ways.

## Exclusions

Normal wear items such as tires, chains, brake pads and cables are not covered. Damage from crashes, improper assembly, commercial or rental use, or aftermarket modifications is excluded. If your frame is damaged in a crash, our Crash Replacement offer gives the original owner 30% off a replacement frame.
