# Trade-In Program

Ready for an upgrade? Bring your current bike to any Nimbus Bikes store and trade it in for store credit.

## How much credit you get

Nimbus bikes under 5 years old earn up to 40% of the original purchase price as store credit. Bikes from other brands earn up to 25% of their current used market value. The exact amount depends on condition, mileage and the age of the components.

## Inspection

A mechanic inspects the bike while you wait. The trade-in inspection takes about 20 minutes and covers the frame, fork, wheels, drivetrain and brakes. For e-bikes, the battery health must be above 70% to qualify. Bikes with cracked frames are not accepted, but we will happily take them through the ReCycle program instead.

## Using your credit

Trade-in credit is valid for 90 days and can be used on any new bike, accessories or service. Credit is issued as a digital store card and can be split across several purchases. It cannot be exchanged for cash.

## What happens to traded bikes

Traded bikes are tuned, cleaned and sold in our certified used section with a 6 month component warranty. Bikes that are not worth refurbishing are stripped for spare parts.
