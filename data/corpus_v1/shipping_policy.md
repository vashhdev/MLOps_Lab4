# Shipping Policy

Nimbus Bikes ships every order from our warehouse in Portland, Oregon.

## Rates and speed

Standard shipping is free on all orders over $99 and costs $8.95 on smaller accessory orders. Standard delivery takes 3-5 business days within the continental United States. Express shipping costs $24.95 and delivers in 1-2 business days. Orders placed before 1 pm Pacific time on a business day ship the same day.

## Bikes in the box

Bikes ship 85% assembled in our recyclable NimbusBox. Final assembly takes about 30 minutes: you attach the handlebar, front wheel, pedals and seatpost. The box includes a 4 mm and a 5 mm hex key, a torque guide and a printed quick start card. If you would rather skip assembly, choose in-store pickup at any Nimbus Bikes location and our mechanics will build the bike for free.

## Remote and international destinations

Shipping a bike to Alaska or Hawaii adds a $150 surcharge. We ship to Canada for a flat $89 with delivery in 7-10 business days, and duties are included in that price. We cannot ship bikes to PO boxes or to freight forwarders.

## Tracking and damage

You will receive a tracking link by email as soon as your order leaves the warehouse. If your box arrives damaged, take photos before opening it and contact support within 48 hours so we can file a claim with the carrier. Do not refuse the delivery, because refused boxes take up to three weeks to return to Portland.
