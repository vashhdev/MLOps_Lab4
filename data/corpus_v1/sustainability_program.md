# Sustainability and the ReCycle Program

Bikes are good for the planet, and we want the way we build and sell them to be good too.

## ReCycle

The ReCycle program accepts any bike of any brand, plus old parts, tires and tubes, at all three stores. Riders get $20 store credit for every complete bike dropped off through ReCycle. Rideable bikes are refurbished by volunteers and donated to the Open Road Youth Project, which runs after-school riding clubs. Since 2016 we have donated more than 2,400 bikes. Bikes that cannot be repaired are stripped, and the aluminum and steel are sent to a local metal recycler.

## Packaging

Every NimbusBox is made from 100% recycled cardboard. We removed all foam from our packaging in 2021 and now use molded paper pulp to protect frames during shipping.

## Energy and giving

Solar panels on the Portland workshop roof supply about 60% of the electricity we use there. Each year we donate 1% of annual sales to local trail building and bike lane advocacy groups in Oregon, Washington and Colorado.

## Batteries

E-bike batteries from any brand can be dropped off for free recycling. We work with a certified battery recycler that recovers lithium, cobalt and nickel for reuse.
