# Our Story

Nimbus Bikes was founded in 2011 by Priya Natarajan and Tom Becker in a rented garage on SE Division Street in Portland, Oregon. Priya was a mechanical engineer who designed carbon parts for aircraft, and Tom ran a small repair stand at a farmers market. They wanted to build bikes that were easy to live with every day and easy to fix.

## Milestones

- 2012: The first Cirrus road bike shipped to 40 customers, all of them local.
- 2015: The Seattle store opened on Westlake Avenue.
- 2016: The ReCycle program started, collecting old bikes for refurbishment and donation.
- 2018: The Denver store opened on Blake Street.
- 2020: The Nimbus Volt e-bike launched and sold out its first production run in six weeks.
- 2022: Nimbus Bikes became a certified B Corporation.

## Today

Nimbus Bikes employs 85 people across Portland, Seattle and Denver. Every frame is still designed in Portland, and the main workshop sits behind the flagship store on SE Alder Street. Priya leads product design, while Tom oversees the stores and the service departments.

## What we believe

We think a bike should last for decades. That is why we publish repair guides for every model, keep spare parts in stock for at least ten years after a model is discontinued, and train our mechanics to fix rather than replace whenever it is safe to do so.
