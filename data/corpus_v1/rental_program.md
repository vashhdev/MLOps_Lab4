# Bike Rentals

Want to try a Nimbus before you buy, or need a bike for a weekend trip? Rentals are available at our Portland and Denver stores.

## Rates

- Standard bike (Cirrus, Stratus or Altus): $45 per day or $220 per week.
- Nimbus Volt e-bike rental costs $75 per day or $350 per week.
- Kids bikes: $20 per day.

Every rental includes a helmet, a lock and a small repair kit with a spare tube.

## Deposit and requirements

A refundable deposit of $200 per bike is held on a credit card and released when the bike comes back undamaged. Renters must be 16 or older, and riders under 18 need a parent or guardian to sign the rental agreement. Please bring a photo ID.

## Pickup and return

Bikes can be picked up from opening time and must be returned by 7 pm. Late returns are charged $15 per hour. Weekly rentals can be extended by phone if the bike is not already reserved.

## Rent then buy

If you buy a new bike within 14 days of your rental, up to three days of rental fees are credited toward the purchase. Summit members receive four free rental days per year as part of their membership.
