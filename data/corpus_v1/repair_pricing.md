# Repair and Service Pricing

Prices effective February 1, 2024 at every Nimbus Bikes service department (Portland, Seattle and Denver). Prices cover labor; parts are extra unless noted.

## Service packages

- Basic Tune-Up ($79): gear and brake adjustment, light wheel truing, drivetrain wipe-down, bolt torque check and a 20-point safety inspection.
- Full Overhaul ($189): complete disassembly, deep cleaning of the frame and drivetrain, new cables and housing (included in the price), bearing service in the hubs, headset and bottom bracket, followed by a full tune-up.
- E-Bike Service ($119): everything in the Basic Tune-Up plus a motor and battery health report for the Nimbus Volt.

## Individual repairs

- Flat tire repair: $15 labor plus $9 for a new tube.
- Hydraulic brake bleed: $45 per brake.
- Wheel truing: $30 per wheel.
- Nimbus Volt diagnostic and firmware update: $65.
- Derailleur hanger alignment: $20.

## Turnaround time

Standard repairs are completed within 3 business days of drop-off. Rush service costs an additional $40 and gets your bike back within 24 hours, subject to parts availability. We always call before doing any work that adds more than $25 to your quote.

## Memberships and warranty work

Chain and Summit members receive discounts on labor and priority scheduling; see the Membership Tiers page for details. Repairs approved under the warranty policy are free of charge and do not count toward the turnaround estimate above.
