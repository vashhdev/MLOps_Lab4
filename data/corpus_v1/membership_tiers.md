# Nimbus Rewards Membership Tiers

Nimbus Rewards has three tiers. You can join or upgrade in any store or online at nimbusbikes.example/rewards.

## Spoke (free)

Spoke is free for everyone. Members earn 1 point per $1 spent, receive 5% off accessories and get invitations to monthly community rides.

## Chain ($49 per year)

The Chain membership costs $49 per year. It includes 10% off accessories and service labor, one free basic tune-up per year, and free flat repairs on bikes bought from Nimbus Bikes.

## Summit ($129 per year)

The Summit membership costs $129 per year. Summit members get 15% off accessories, apparel and service labor, two free basic tune-ups each year, priority repair with a 48-hour turnaround, and four free rental days per year at Portland or Denver.

## Points

Points never expire while your membership is active. Every 500 points can be redeemed for a $25 reward. Rewards cannot be used on gift cards or applied to financing payments.

## Cancellation

Paid tiers renew automatically each year. You can cancel within 14 days of renewal for a full refund; after that the tier stays active until the end of the membership year. Downgrading to Spoke keeps all of your points.
