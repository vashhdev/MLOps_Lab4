# Store Locations and Hours

Nimbus Bikes has three retail stores. Each one has a full service department, a short test ride loop and a fitting area.

## Locations

- Portland (flagship): 1420 SE Alder Street, Portland, OR 97214. Our original store and home of the main workshop.
- Seattle: 233 Westlake Avenue North, Seattle, WA 98109. Two hours of free parking validation in the Westlake garage.
- Denver: 1875 Blake Street, Denver, CO 80202. Opened in 2018, a short walk from Union Station.

## Opening hours

All stores keep the same opening hours unless noted:

- Monday to Friday: 10 am to 7 pm
- Saturday: 9 am to 6 pm
- Sunday: 11 am to 5 pm

The Denver store is closed on Mondays from November through February, when winter riding slows down.

## Holidays

All stores close on Thanksgiving Day, December 25 and January 1. On December 24 and December 31 the stores close early at 3 pm.

## Service desk

The service desk stops taking drop-offs one hour before closing so our mechanics can finish check-in paperwork. Same-day pickups must be collected before the store closes, otherwise the bike stays safely locked in the workshop overnight.
